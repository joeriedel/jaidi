jaidi changelog
===============

0.2.5 (2026-10-14)
------------------

A fix on Imperial City, and better aim assist.

- Fixed: on Imperial City, shooting the switch in the slot behind the high ledge did
  nothing.
- Fixed: the aim assist could bend your shots toward enemies you couldn't see, such as
  ones behind walls or pillars. Now it only aims at enemies in view.

Still missing: settings and configuration screens.

0.2.4 (2026-10-14)
------------------

A drawing fix in levels.

- Fixed: rendering bug on Jabba's ship.

Still missing: settings and configuration screens.

0.2.3 (2026-10-14)
------------------

Better Enhanced cinematics, and a fix to dying.

- Enhanced mode: wipes, slides and fades between cinematic shots now fill the wide
  screen, instead of dropping back to the 4:3 picture while they run.
- Enhanced mode: a few shots stay at 4:3 where filling the wide screen would cut off
  heads or subtitles (Mohc and Vader at Madine's office, the Jabba pit, the award
  ceremony). The rest still fill it.
- Enhanced mode: backgrounds behind a widened shot now widen with it (the hall at the
  start of the Madine cinematic no longer stops short of the right edge).
- Enhanced mode: smooth motion between a cinematic's frames now falls back to the
  original frames where it can't follow the art, instead of showing doubled or smeared
  pictures (the ship after the Research Facility, the explosion ring in the Robotics
  Facility cinematic, the guard at the Jabba pit).
- Enhanced mode: fixed a thin light line along the edges of talking heads, such as Mohc's.
- Fixed: you could still fire and use things while dying.

Still missing: settings and configuration screens.

0.2.2 (2026-10-13)
------------------

Two drawing fixes in levels.

- Fixed: mines and bodies lying on the floor showed with their bottoms cut flat.
- Enhanced mode: floors and ceilings of rotating sectors now turn with them (like the
  catwalk in the Research Facility). Original mode leaves them fixed, as the original game does.

Still missing: settings and configuration screens.

0.2.1 (2026-10-13)
------------------

The original's cheat codes, and fixes from playing 0.2.0.

- All 27 original cheat codes (see README).
- Finishing a mission opens the next one on the agent menu right away and selects it, as
  the original does.
- Fixed: you could walk through Kyle's ship at the start of the sewers.
- Fixed: walls missing from the platform booth in the sewers when seen from inside.

Not there yet: settings and configuration screens.

0.2.0 (2026-10-13)
------------------

Mostly the 3D view: skies, 3D objects and the edges between sectors now draw as in the
original, and Enhanced mode lights levels in full color. Also a Pause key and a debug panel.

- The Pause key pauses a level or a cinematic, with its music and sound, until it's pressed
  again.
- The Insert key opens a debug panel: where you are, your stats and inventory, the enemies,
  frame times, and in cinematics the scene and its timing, with stepping and scrubbing. Pause
  a level to use it with the mouse; it also has god mode and give everything.
- Enhanced mode lights levels in full color from the game's own colormaps, smoothly, without
  the headlamp's rings, and keeps their tints in the dark (Talay's walls go burnt orange).
- 3D objects lit and clipped as in the original, their textures the right way up: Kyle's
  ship over Talay shows whole and textured, and floor-like surfaces (the shuttle deck on
  Detention Center) stay fixed instead of sliding.
- Fixed: jumping into a sky showed the ceiling instead of the sky, and floors beyond showed
  over the walls' tops.
- Fixed: a strip of wall drawn up through Talay's sky near an opening, and a vertical line
  while walking there.
- Fixed: a flickering floor on Testbase, and ships flying over walls cut off by them.

Still missing: settings and configuration screens.

0.1.0 (2026-10-12)
------------------

Preliminary release, generally feature complete, missing settings and configuration screens though. I am unsure how I want to do this exactly since the original game's settings, if I recall correctly, used a separate setup app for that. And I don't even remember if keys were actually configurable either.

- Full campaign: cinematics, briefings, agent menu, Esc menu.
- All INF scripting: doors, lifts, switches, keys, teleporters, moving walls and floors.
- PDA (F1) and the map overlay (Tab).
- Headlamp, goggles, ice cleats and gas mask (F2 to F5).
- All ten weapons, secondary fire included.
- All enemies and bosses, including the dark troopers, Mohc and Boba Fett.
- OPL3 music using the original FM driver's instruments, plus sound effects and voices.
- Enhanced mode (F12, on by default): levels at your screen's resolution; cinematics redrawn with smooth motion, in-between frames, upscaled art and widescreen.
- Windows and Linux.
